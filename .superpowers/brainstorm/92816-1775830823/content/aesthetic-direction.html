Aesthetic Direction

The mockup spec goes for "Linear/Vercel/Raycast" — dark premium, near-black, blue accents. What direction do you want to push toward?

A

Darker, More Refined — "Midnight Studio"

Push the mockup's direction further. Even deeper blacks (#06060a), sharper blue accents, tighter spacing, more premium feel. Think: Zed editor meets Linear. Very serious, very focused.

B

Warm Dark — "Amber Studio"

Dark background but swap the cold blue palette for warmer amber/gold accents. Feels more creative and personal, less corporate. Think: Bear app, Craft, Obsidian with a warm tint.

C

High Contrast — "Ink & Neon"

True black (#000) backgrounds, vivid neon accents (electric blue, bright green), heavy typographic hierarchy. Feels bold and developer-native. Think: Warp terminal, Fig, or early Vercel.

D

Muted + Sophisticated — "Graphite"

Pull back on saturation everywhere. Warm gray backgrounds, very subtle teal/slate accents, lots of negative space. Feels editorial and calm. Think: Notion dark, Arc sidebar, early Figma.
