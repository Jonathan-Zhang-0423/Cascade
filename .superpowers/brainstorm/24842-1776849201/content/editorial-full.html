Design decisions
1
Dashboard: cream on dark shell
The light cream phone pops against the dark page background — it reads as premium, like a physical object. The Lora serif wordmark gives Cascade an editorial identity without being precious.
2
In-project: dark warm, not cold blue-gray
The IDE panel shifts to #1a1916 (warm near-black) instead of the current cold #0c0c14. Amber (#c8a84a) replaces blue as the accent — consistent with the dashboard's palette. The AI brand color is now amber throughout both screens.
3
Plan card replaces raw step list
A bordered card groups the build steps — amber dot for done, pulsing outline for active, dim dot for pending. This gives spatial context at a glance without opening a separate panel.
4
Preview CTA becomes a full-width button
The floating pill is replaced by a pinned amber gradient bar above the input — can't be obscured by chat messages, and matches the "Start Something New" button energy on the dashboard.
